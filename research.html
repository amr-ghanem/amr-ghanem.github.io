Lines of work

Five threads, and the papers behind each.

Multi-hazard fragility

A fragility curve answers one question about one structure. This work asks it across a whole parameter space, and for hazards that arrive in sequence rather than alone — an earthquake, then the tsunami behind it, where the first changes what the second meets.

Accessibility and inclusive construction

Simulation applied to who can actually get out of a building, and what it costs to make one usable. Evacuation under heterogeneous mobility, resource allocation for accessible facilities, and life-cycle assessment of inclusive construction. Led from King Saud University.

  • 2025Toward sustainable inclusive construction: A comprehensive life cycle assessment of disability needs requirements in civil engineering projects
  • 2025Enhancing accessibility for people with disabilities through upgrades in construction facilities in Saudi Arabia: A quantitative assessment
  • 2025Inclusive crowd evacuation modeling under heterogeneous mobility constraints
  • 2025Optimizing accessibility utilizing simulation-based framework for efficient resource allocation and scheduling for disability-friendly utilities

Sustainable structural materials

Whether recycled plastic can replace conventional aggregate in structural lightweight concrete — assessed on performance, cost and environmental impact together, rather than one at a time.

  • 2023Green lightweight concrete utilizing sustainable processed recycled plastic aggregates: Technical, economic and environmental assessment
  • 2022Assessment of sustainable green lightweight concrete incorporated in new construction technologies

Journal articles

Effects of tsunami wave and bathymetric parameters on hydrodynamic forces and structural fragility

Abdelhafeez, M., Park, H., Sherif, M., Ghanem, A., & Moon, D.-S.

Journal of Building Engineering2026

The seafloor decides how hard the water hits. Wave and bathymetric parameters are varied systematically to show how much the force reaching a building changes with the shape of the approach.

Toward sustainable inclusive construction: A comprehensive life cycle assessment of disability needs requirements in civil engineering projects

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M., Abdelhafeez, M. H., Ghanem, A., & Shafaay, M. A.

Journal of Disability Research2025Open access

A full life cycle assessment of what accessibility requirements actually cost across a project, environmentally as well as financially.

Enhancing accessibility for people with disabilities through upgrades in construction facilities in Saudi Arabia: A quantitative assessment

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M., Abdelhafeez, M. H., Ghanem, A., Shafaay, M. A., & Alkahtani, B. N.

Journal of Disability Research2025Open access

A quantitative survey of accessibility in Saudi construction facilities, establishing a baseline that upgrade programmes can be measured against.

Optimizing accessibility utilizing simulation-based framework for efficient resource allocation and scheduling for disability-friendly utilities

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M., Ghanem, A., Abdelhafeez, M., & Shafaay, M. A.

Scientific Reports2025Open access

A simulation framework for scheduling and allocating resources on accessibility upgrades, so limited budgets go where they remove the most friction.

Fragility curve-based resilience index for structural vulnerability assessment in hazard analysis

Sherif, M. A., Ghanem, A. M., Abdelhafeez, M. H., Jung, D., Kim, D.-J., & Moon, D.-S.

Smart Structures and Systems2025

A fragility curve shows how failure probability grows with intensity, but it does not say which of two structures is more vulnerable. This condenses the whole curve into a single index so structures can be ranked for mitigation priority.

Inclusive crowd evacuation modeling under heterogeneous mobility constraints

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M., Ghanem, A., & Abdelhafeez, M.

Scientific Reports2025Open access

Evacuation models usually assume everyone moves at the same speed. This one does not, and the difference changes how long a building takes to clear.

Seismic vulnerability of reinforced concrete frame structures: Obtaining plan or vertical mass irregularity from structure use change

Ghanem, A., Lee, Y.-J., & Moon, D.-S.

Journal of Structural Engineering2024

When a building's use changes — an office floor becomes storage — mass irregularity appears where the original designer never accounted for it. This quantifies what that does to seismic vulnerability.

Green lightweight concrete utilizing sustainable processed recycled plastic aggregates: Technical, economic and environmental assessment

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M. A., & Ghanem, A. M.

Construction and Building Materials2023

Whether recycled plastic can replace conventional aggregate in structural lightweight concrete, assessed on all three of performance, cost and environmental impact.

Assessment of sustainable green lightweight concrete incorporated in new construction technologies

Alqahtani, F. K., Sherif, M. A., Ghanem, A. M., & Abotaleb, I. S.

KSCE Journal of Civil Engineering2022

How green lightweight concrete behaves inside modern construction methods rather than in isolation, which is where most material studies stop.
